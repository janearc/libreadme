# Evidence

The numbers in `profile/profiles/reference-dark-2026-09-03.json` were not
chosen. They were measured, on 2026-09-03, by putting rendered specimens in
front of the reference reader in the browser and on the screen she reads in
every day, and asking which she could read. This directory is the record.

A number without its measurement is an opinion. Keep these with the profile.

                     spacing, weight and size (F)
    specimen2.html   round two: the section label five ways, size on its
                     own at 20, 24 and 28, and weight 400 against 500 at 24
    specimen3.html   round three: the same paragraph at six brightnesses,
                     from 16.4 to 1 down to 6.8 to 1, to find the ceiling
