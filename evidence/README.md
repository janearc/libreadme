# Evidence

The numbers in `profile/profiles/reference-dark-2026-09-03.json` were not
chosen. They were measured, on 2026-09-03, by putting rendered specimens in
front of the reference reader in the browser and on the screen she reads in
every day, and asking which she could read. This directory is the record.

A number without its measurement is an opinion. Keep these with the profile.

## What is here

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `specimen.html` | round one: six blocks, one change each, from what shipped (A) through contrast, line height, letter spacing, weight and size (F) |
| `specimen2.html` | round two: the section label five ways, size on its own at 20, 24 and 28, and weight 400 against 500 at 24 |
| `specimen3.html` | round three: the same paragraph at six brightnesses from 16.4 to 1 down to 6.8 to 1, to find the ceiling and the floor; and the label without amber |
| `ratings.md` | what the reader said about each, in her words, and which number each answer became |

Open the pages in a browser to see what was rated. They are self-contained
and load no fonts or scripts from anywhere.

## How to read the method

Each round changed one thing at a time and asked for comfort, not taste.
The contrast band came from round three: the brightest paragraph that did
not glare and the dimmest that could still be read at length, with the
single most comfortable one as the centre. Size, weight, line height and
letter spacing came from rounds one and two. The hue rule came from a
status chip that passed the contrast ratio and could not be read; that
finding is recorded in the profile's note rather than in a specimen,
because it was measured on a live page.

## Re-measuring

`libreadme measure` regenerates ladders of the same kind and writes a new
snapshot from the answers. It never edits this one.
