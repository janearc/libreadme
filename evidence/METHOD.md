# Method

## How to read the method

Each round changed one thing at a time and asked for comfort, not taste. The
contrast band came from round three: the brightest paragraph that did not
glare and the dimmest that could still be read at length, with the single
most comfortable one as the centre.

Size, weight, line height and letter spacing came from rounds one and two.
The hue rule came from a status chip that passed the contrast ratio and
could not be read. It was measured on a live page, not in a specimen, so
the finding is recorded in the profile's note.

## Re-measuring

`libreadme measure` regenerates ladders of the same kind and writes a new
snapshot from the answers. It never edits this one.
